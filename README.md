# Privacy Policy for Plugins Hub: Payment & Rules

**Last Updated: May 8, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Plugins Hub: Payment & Rules ("the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you install or use the App in connection with your Shopify-supported store.

## 1. Personal Information the App Collects
Plugins Hub: Payment & Rules is built using Shopify Functions. We prioritize merchant and customer privacy:
- **No Customer Data Collection:** The App does not collect, store, or transmit any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from your customers.
- **Shopify Infrastructure:** All payment customization logic is executed entirely within Shopify's secure infrastructure.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the configuration data you provide in the App admin (such as payment rules and cart thresholds) solely to provide the payment customization service to your store.

## 3. Data Retention
Since we do not store customer data, there is no retention of customer information. Your app configuration settings are stored within the Shopify environment and are deleted if you uninstall the App.
